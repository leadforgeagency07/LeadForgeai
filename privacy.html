Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LeadForge AI ("LeadForge AI," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Hassan Allam, respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects, what it doesn't, and how it's handled.

1. Information We Collect Directly

This website does not contain any forms, account sign-ups, or direct data-collection tools. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information through this site itself.

2. Booking a Consultation

When you click "Book a Demo," you are taken to our scheduling page hosted by Calendly, a third-party service. Any information you submit there (such as your name, email, or phone number) is collected and processed by Calendly directly, under their own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review at calendly.com/privacy. We receive the booking details you choose to share through that process in order to prepare for and conduct our consultation call with you.

3. Fonts and Third-Party Resources

This site loads typography from Google Fonts. As a result, your browser makes a request to Google's servers to retrieve these font files, which may involve your IP address being shared with Google as part of that standard request. We do not control this data flow beyond using the font service itself. You can read more about how Google handles this at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, or advertising trackers of its own.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for business use by clinic owners and decision-makers, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site or our services evolve. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to leadforge.agency07@gmail.com.
